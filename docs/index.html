The Method

Basis = purchase + rehab/improvements + buying costs. Gain = net sale price (after selling costs) − basis. Short-term gain is taxed at your ordinary rate; a sale after more than 12 months at your long-term rate. State tax and NIIT are applied to both paths, so they do not fake a saving. Holding cash subtracts every month of carrying plus one-off hold costs. The break-even future price is solved exactly — it is the price where holding and selling today leave you with identical cash. Not tax advice; brackets, depreciation and dealer-vs-investor status change real bills — confirm with your CPA before you time a sale around a date.
